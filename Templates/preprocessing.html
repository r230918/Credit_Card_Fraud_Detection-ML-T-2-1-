1. RobustScaler Transformation (Time & Amount)

Why standard Z-score normalization fails on transaction amounts.

The PCA features (V1 through V28) are already standardized through Principal Component Analysis. However, Time and Amount contain extreme high-value outliers (e.g. multi-thousand dollar transactions).

RobustScaler Formula:
x_scaled = (x - Median(x)) / IQR(x)
Where IQR is the Interquartile Range (75th percentile − 25th percentile).
  • Prevents extreme financial amounts from dominating gradient calculations in Logistic Regression and XGBoost.
  • Guarantees identical feature scale without compressing non-outlier distributions.
